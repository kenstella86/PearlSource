Four cities. One supply chain.

The Pearl River Delta is the densest manufacturing cluster on earth. We chose to go deep here — not wide across China.

Guangzhou — 广州

What it makes: Apparel, cosmetics, auto parts, medical devices, leather goods, watches.

Why buyers come here: Canton Fair twice a year, largest wholesale markets (Baiyun, Shahe), and a deep apparel supply chain.

Explore Guangzhou →

Foshan — 佛山

What it makes: Ceramics, furniture, aluminium profiles, home appliances, hardware, stainless steel.

Why buyers come here: World's largest ceramic tile cluster, Nanhai furniture, and a 1-hour drive from our office.

Explore Foshan →

Shenzhen — 深圳

What it makes: Consumer electronics, 3C, smart hardware, PCBs, new energy, drones, cross-border e-commerce supply chain.

Why buyers come here: The global hardware capital. If you're building a gadget, this is where you come.

Explore Shenzhen →

Dongguan — 东莞

What it makes: Precision molds, injection molding, toys, footwear, bags, contract manufacturing.

Why buyers come here: "The factory of the world" for mid-volume contract manufacturing. Strong on tooling and quality.

Explore Dongguan →
