Frequently asked

Pricing questions

Why is the initial match free?

Because we'd rather earn your trust with a good shortlist than charge you upfront. If our 3 recommendations don't fit, you haven't lost anything. If you move forward with an audit or agent service, we get paid for real work.

Do factories pay you?

No. We refuse paid placement, referral fees, or "commission per introduction" from factories. Our income comes entirely from buyers. This is the only way our recommendation stays honest.

What if I need more than 3 factories?

We find 3 because that's the number a buyer can realistically evaluate. If none fit, we'll send 3 more free — same product, different angle. We won't pad the list to look busy.

Is the 5% agent fee negotiable?

For orders above $50,000, we can discuss a sliding scale (e.g., 4% on orders $50K–$100K, 3% above $100K). Smaller orders stay at 5% — it's the minimum that lets us do the job properly.
