What we refuse to do

Three promises we will never break

1. We never take paid placement

If a factory offers us money to appear higher or to be recommended to you, we refuse. Our income comes from you — the buyer. That's the only way our recommendation stays honest.

2. We never blast your RFQ

Your product spec, target price and company details go to at most 3 factories we've already vetted. You won't wake up to 47 WhatsApp messages from random suppliers.

3. We never list a factory we haven't visited

Our "visited in person" count is real. If we haven't been to the floor, seen the production lines, and met the sales manager face-to-face, the factory doesn't appear on our site.
