From product brief to 3 vetted factories in 48 hours.

Here's exactly what happens after you submit your request — no vague sales talk, just the steps.

Day 0 — You

Step 1: Submit your brief (90 seconds)

Fill 6 fields on our form: product name, quantity, target price, required certifications, destination market, your email. That's it. No account, no long questionnaire.

Day 0–1 — Us

Step 2: We screen our database

Our team filters the visited-factory list by product category, capacity, certification and location (Guangzhou / Foshan / Shenzhen / Dongguan). We shortlist 8–10 candidates.

Day 1–2 — Us

Step 3: We call and verify

We contact the shortlisted factories directly in Chinese. We confirm current capacity, MOQ flexibility, export markets, lead time and whether they accept your order size. We reject 40–60% at this stage.

Day 2 — You

Step 4: You receive the shortlist

An email with a comparison sheet: 3 factories, each with company profile, main products, capacity, certifications, estimated price range, lead time, and our notes on fit. Plus links to our on-site photos.

Day 3+ — You choose

Step 5: You pick one (or all three)

We introduce you directly to the sales manager (usually via WeChat / email / video call). You ask for samples, negotiate, and decide. We stay in the loop if you want us to.

Optional — Ongoing

Step 6: We manage production (if you want)

Once you place an order, our end-to-end agent service kicks in: sample follow-up, production monitoring, pre-shipment inspection, shipping coordination. 5% of order value.

What you get back

The 48-hour package

📋 Comparison sheet

A side-by-side PDF: price range, MOQ, lead time, certifications, main export markets, and our fit rating (good / fair / not recommended).

📸 On-site evidence

Links to photos and short video clips we took at each factory — production floor, warehouse, office. Not the polished catalog shots.

💬 Direct intro

Warm intro email to the sales manager at each factory, with your brief already translated into Chinese. You skip the cold-email phase.
