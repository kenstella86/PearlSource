A small team based in Foshan, working for buyers worldwide.

We are not a big platform. We are a local sourcing team — the kind you'd hire if you flew to China yourself and needed someone on the ground.

Where we are

Our office is in Foshan, Guangdong — right in the middle of the Pearl River Delta manufacturing belt. From here:

  • 30 minutes to Foshan's ceramic and furniture clusters
  • 1 hour to Guangzhou's trade markets and apparel factories
  • 1.5 hours to Dongguan's mold and precision parts factories
  • 2 hours to Shenzhen's electronics supply chain

When a problem comes up at a factory, we are 2 hours away — not 12 time zones.

What we do

We do three things, and we do them well:

  1. Visit factories. We drive to the floor, meet the managers, see the production lines, take photos.
  2. Match buyers to factories. We filter our visited list against your product, MOQ and budget.
  3. Follow orders. If you want, we monitor production, inspect pre-shipment, and coordinate shipping.

We don't do marketplaces, paid rankings, or RFQ blasts. That's not what we're here for.
