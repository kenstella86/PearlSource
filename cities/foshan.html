Foshan — ceramics, furniture & hardware capital.

Foshan is 30 minutes from our office. We visit factories here every week — this is the city we know best.

Key clusters

What Foshan makes

Porcelain & tiles

Chancheng & Nanhai districts. Foshan produces ~30% of the world's ceramic tiles. Major brands: Marco Polo, Dongpeng, New Pearl. Also strong in sanitary ware and art ceramics.

Furniture

Longjiang & Dayi towns (Shunde). "Chinese furniture capital." Strong in upholstered sofas, dining sets, bedroom furniture, and custom hospitality projects.

Aluminium profiles

Dali town (Nanhai). Windows, doors, curtain wall, industrial profiles. Deep supply chain for extrusion, anodizing, powder coating.

Home appliances

Shunde district. Midea and Galanz are headquartered here. Strong OEM/ODM base for small appliances, kitchen appliances, refrigerators.

Stainless steel

Chencun town (Shunde). "Stainless steel capital of China." Sheets, pipes, custom fabrication for commercial kitchen and architecture.

Hardware & lighting

Nanhai. Door handles, locks, fasteners, and decorative lighting. Strong export-oriented SMEs.

Practical tips for buyers

Logistics

30 min to Guangzhou Nansha port, 1 hour to Huangpu port. Most Foshan factories quote FOB Guangzhou. Inland trucking to Shenzhen/Yantian is also common for exports to the US West Coast.

MOQ reality

Tile MOQ is usually one 20ft container per model. Furniture MOQ is 5–20 pieces per SKU depending on customization. Smaller runs are possible but price per unit rises — we'll flag this on your shortlist.

Factory vs. trader

Foshan has both. The big tile brands are real factories. Around Lecong furniture market, many showrooms are traders. We mark on every shortlist which is which.

Best time to visit

March/April and October/November, aligned with Canton Fair. If you can't fly, we visit on your behalf and send photos and video.
